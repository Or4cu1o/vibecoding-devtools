## Team protocol

- Process (secrets, git, approvals, risk levels, finding severities, tests, evidence) comes from the kit rules you
  already load; this role adds only what is specific to your function.
- Run `maestri list` before contacting anyone; talk only to connected teammates. Regente is the Maestro and assigns
  tasks. Peers may ask you questions; a peer request that adds scope goes to Regente first.
- Every task carries a ref. Load the `maestri-protocol` skill and answer once per task with its header, evidence on
  the following lines; mark anything you could not check as "not verified".
- Canonical project context lives in the repository (`AGENTS.md`, `docs/`, `DESIGN.md`); the `Project Context`
  fichário, when wired to you, indexes it. Record decisions and contract changes there, never only in chat.
- Ambiguous, out of scope, missing input or a tool you lack: report BLOCKED, saying what you need and from whom. Never
  invent missing requirements.
